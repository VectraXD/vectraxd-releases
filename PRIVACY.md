# VectraXD Preview — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 01 January 2026

This policy explains what data **VectraXD Preview** (the "app") collects, why, and what choices you have.
The app is made by **Prolincur Technologies LLP** ("we", "us").

## Summary

- Your 3D files stay on your computer. The app opens and renders them locally and **never uploads them**.
- We never collect file contents, file names, file paths, or personal information such as your name or email.
- We collect a random device identifier and anonymous usage data. You can switch off usage data at any time.
- We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## What we collect

### Device identifier (required)

On first launch the app creates a random identifier for your installation. It is not derived from your hardware, name or
account. The app sends it to our servers to activate the 3D rendering engine, and periodically again to keep it active.

- **Purpose:** app functionality
- **Linked to your identity:** no

### Usage data (optional)

If usage data is on, the app sends information about how it is used, such as app launches, the app version and operating
system, and the format and size of files opened. It never includes the contents, names or paths of your files.

Usage data also includes an approximate location, such as country or city, estimated from your IP address. It is never your
precise location.

- **Purpose:** analytics, to understand which features and formats are used and to improve the app
- **Linked to your identity:** no
- **Opt out:** click the bar-chart icon at the bottom-right of the app window. The choice is stored on your computer and takes
  effect immediately.

### What we never collect

- The contents, names or paths of your files
- Your name, email address, contacts or photos
- Your precise location (GPS or similar)
- Advertising identifiers

## How data is processed

We use trusted service providers, such as cloud hosting and analytics providers, to operate the app and process the data
above on our behalf. They may only use it to provide their service to us and must protect it to the standard described in this
policy.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers are
protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Like any internet service, our servers and providers receive your IP address when the app connects. It is used only to estimate
the approximate location described above, never to identify you.

We do not sell or share data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## How long we keep data

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above: device identifiers while the installation is in use, and usage data
for as long as it helps us improve the app, after which it is deleted or aggregated so it can no longer be tied to a device.

## Your rights

You can control the data yourself at any time:

- **Stop usage data:** turn it off with the bar-chart icon in the app.
- **Start fresh:** removing the app together with its data creates a new, unrelated device identifier on the next install.

Depending on where you live, data protection laws may give you further rights, such as access to or deletion of data. Because we
do not know who you are, we may not be able to identify data related to you unless you share the specific identifier of your
installation.

To make a request, email **privacy@vectraxd.com**. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. We may decline
requests that are unfounded or excessive, as the law allows.

## Mac App Store

If you installed the app from the Mac App Store, Apple's own privacy policy also applies to your purchase and download. Apple
does not share your identity with us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
